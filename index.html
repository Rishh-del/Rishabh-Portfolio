01 · the story

a blurb about me

I'm Rishabh, a computer science engineer who spent his first job asking one question: can this system be trusted?

I was born in New Delhi and grew up across Germany, Singapore, the Netherlands, India and Malaysia. Going to international schools taught me early that the same idea can be explained in many different ways, and that the best one is usually the one the other person can actually use.

After a B.Tech in Computer Science, I did two data science internships, building a sales forecasting tool and a reader-prediction model for a national newspaper. Then I joined EY's Tech Risk Assurance team, where I have tested 100+ IT controls across 7+ industries and sat in front of clients on a regular basis.

Along the way I noticed where I get the most energy: figuring out where a product fails a real person, and what should change. That's why I'm moving into product. I'm especially drawn to fintech and payments, where the product decides whether someone's money stays safe.

Outside work, I write. Since my first year of college I've written 18+ stories (horror, jungle adventure and psychological thrillers), and I'm happiest in a cinema, asking why a scene was written the way it was. It's the same habit I bring to products.

100+IT controls independently evaluated
300+coffee chats with PMs and consultants
10+independent product case studies
7+industries worked across

The AI handoff: several PMs told me their job has shifted from writing specs to deciding when a model may act alone and when it must hand over to a human. In fraud and payments, that boundary is the product.
